| `fix(a2a): wait for an in-flight actor operation instead of failing` | a turn sent while another operation held its Actor, such as the repoint of a suspended Actor onto a newer template right after a Substrate upgrade, failed: Substrate's router resumes the Actor on the turn's dial, ate-api answered `Aborted: another operation is in progress for this actor` to every resume of its parking budget, and the router refused the turn, which reached the caller as a refused send although the same turn was answered seconds later (giantswarm/kagent-upstream#191). A send the runtime refused without taking its input, on a runtime that is not lost, now makes the gateway resume the Actor itself through ate-api, waiting while it answers `Aborted` (from 500 ms, doubling to 5 s, at most two minutes), and run the send once more; any other answer, or an Actor still held at the bound, keeps the retryable refusal. The gateway waits once per turn. Docs: `docs/architecture/a2a-gateway.md` | to file; queued in giantswarm/giantswarm#37742 by the upstream exit |
