| `fix(session): retry a failed quiesce once the Actor runs again` | the settle of a failed quiesce (the row above it) released the claim without a snapshot as soon as the Actor was `RUNNING` again, so a first `SuspendActor` that outlived its deadline and was abandoned by Substrate (the first suspend after an atelet restart, giantswarm/substrate#162) left the turn without a snapshot for good, and nothing on the session said why (giantswarm/kagent-upstream#213). Stored in the session's protobuf, no migration.
