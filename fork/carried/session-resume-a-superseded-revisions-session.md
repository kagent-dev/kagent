| `fix(session): resume a superseded revision's session on the current one` | the workflow resumed a session's Actor on the runtime revision it was prepared on and refused an Actor of any other ActorTemplate, so a conversation started before its agent was re-rendered by a newer release kept the old revision's template for good: a revision rendered before the egress trust settings (`SSL_CERT_FILE`, `REQUESTS_CA_BUNDLE` → `/run/kagent/egress/trust-bundle.pem`) failed every model call with `x509: certificate signed by unknown authority` once the egress gateway intercepted TLS, while new conversations of the same agent worked, and on an installation upgraded through several releases most READY conversations sat on superseded revisions whose templates, rendered before credential injection, still carried the model credential inline and were never collected (giantswarm/kagent-upstream#170). Before a turn dials a quiesced Actor (under the turn's dispatch reservation), on the Resume RPC of a suspended session, and on the leader's sweep every `KAGENT_REVISION_REPOINT_INTERVAL` (5m; `controller.revisionRepointInterval`; 0 disables) over the settled READY sessions on superseded revisions, an Actor whose agent has a newer current revision in the same atespace is moved onto that revision's ActorTemplate (Substrate's `UpdateActor`, accepted while the Actor is suspended; the resume restores its data onto the new template's golden), given that revision's egress allowlist and recorded as prepared on it, so the superseded revision becomes unreferenced and the runtime revision GC deletes it with its ActorTemplate. The repoint is idempotent at every step, a lifecycle operation that claimed the session meanwhile wins and the Actor goes back, a template Substrate refuses for a different sandbox config or volume layout is logged and the session stays on its revision; a live or paused Actor is never moved. The 1.3 counterpart of `release-1.2`'s giantswarm/kagent-upstream#171 and #190 on the session workflow.
