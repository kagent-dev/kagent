| `feat(session): suspend a runtime paused for input once the pause is older than a TTL` | a turn that ends `input-required`/`auth-required` pauses the actor on its worker's node (`PauseActor`, the upstream design of kagent-dev/kagent#2714) and nothing made that checkpoint durable, so a spot interruption, a consolidation or a node roll while a person took their time lost the session's runtime state: the reply's resume found no worker on the recorded node, Substrate reported the locality miss as `no free workers available`, the task failed after the 60 s resume budget and the instance could neither be resumed, suspended nor deleted (production, 2026-09-14/15, giantswarm/giantswarm#37795). The controller's leader now sweeps READY sessions whose latest task waits for input without a recorded snapshot and, once the pause is older than `KAGENT_PAUSED_RUNTIME_TTL` (2m; `controller.pausedRuntimeTTL`; 0 disables), suspends the actor durably — `SuspendActor` uploads the pause checkpoint to the snapshot store — and records the snapshot as the task's boundary (guarded on the task still waiting, so a reply that landed meanwhile keeps its own boundary); the sweep takes the session's quiesce lock without waiting and the gateway dispatches under the runtime-call lock, so a suspend and a reply never overlap. A reply within the TTL keeps the in-place resume; a later one restores from the external snapshot on any worker. The platform's meta chart leaves the default | superseded structurally by kagent-dev/kagent#2939 and #2970 (idle expiry deletes and skips waiting tasks, so a paused turn is not covered): the patch drops at the re-pin and is rebuilt as `KAGENT_PAUSED_RUNTIME_TTL` on the Session model in the candidate.
